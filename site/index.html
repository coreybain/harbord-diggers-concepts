Ten concepts, two clubs.

Two Christmas advent calendar pages for Harbord Diggers, plus three homepage concepts for each club. Each one uses the club's own photography and explores a different header banner, motion style and mood. Open one, scroll through, and switch between them with the control at the bottom of each page.
